Put down on Saraswati Puja. Picked up on Vijayadasami.

Every year, books and instruments rest before the goddess for a day. The next morning, learning begins again. If there is a flute at home that has only ever been kept, Vijayadasami is the morning it gets played.

Why the flute, at any age?

Vijayadasami is for the beginning. The flute is for the years after.

  • Discipline & Self-Control

    Regular practice builds patience, perseverance and the habit of showing up daily, whether you're in Class 4 or in your forties.

  • Focus & Concentration

    Breath, fingers and ear have to work together. That attention carries over to studies, work and everything after.

  • Creativity & Expression

    Music unlocks creative thinking and gives you a way to express what words don't.

  • Cultural Connection

    Connect with India's rich Carnatic music heritage and the divine tradition of Lord Krishna's flute.

  • Health Benefits

    Flute playing improves breath control and lung capacity, and is a natural stress-buster for growing minds and tired ones.

  • Confidence Building

    Mastering a skill and playing for others builds self-esteem, on a school stage or in your own living room.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why begin on Vijayadasami?

It is the traditional day for Vidyarambham, when families start a child's learning or take up a new instrument or art. This masterclass is timed for it, and it is open to every age.

I can't attend on Vijayadasami. Can I still join?

Yes, there is a masterclass every Sunday at 10 am.

Am I too old, or is my child too young?

No. Students from 7 to 67 have learnt with these methods. There is no age limit to learn this skill. Very young children learn best with a parent alongside. The one thing to get right is the flute itself: its size should suit the player's fingers.

I have zero music background. Is this for me?

Yes. If you have a strong interest and the will to learn, this is definitely for you. The course is structured for self-learners and starts from the first note.

Will Nandakumar teach the class himself?

Yes, all the content is a recorded session by Nandakumar himself, curated from 18 years of teaching experience and structured to make it easy for every self-learner.

Will I have access to the video recording once the course is over?

Yes, you will have access to the course for a maximum of 1 year from the date of purchase. If you are unable to complete it within one year, you can renew your access by buying it again.

Will I get a certificate?

Yes, you will receive a completion certificate once you successfully complete the course and all the assignments.

In which language will the course be taught?

The course is available in English. Hindi, Tamil and Kannada are coming soon, and you will be able to move to the language of your choice free of charge.

Will it really change my life?

Imagine yourself playing the flute every day. Notice how your family calms down when you play. Notice how your neighbours appreciate you when you start playing songs. More than all, notice the change within you when you are playing, deeply focused, calm at the centre. This is nothing short of meditation.

Please drop a note via our contact channels and we will reach out to you to understand and resolve the issue.
